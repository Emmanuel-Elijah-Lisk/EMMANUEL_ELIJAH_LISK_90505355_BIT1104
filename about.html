Our History

Founded in 2020 by former professional footballer John Kamara, our agency emerged from a passion to create pathways for Sierra Leone's talented players. We started in a small office with a big dream and have grown into one of West Africa's most respected football agencies. Our journey has been marked by dedication, integrity, and countless success stories of players achieving their dreams.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading football agency, recognized globally for developing world-class talent from Sierra Leone. We envision a future where every talented Sierra Leonean footballer has equal access to opportunities that match their abilities, regardless of their background. We aim to put Sierra Leone on the global football map and inspire the next generation.

Our Mission

To discover, nurture, and represent exceptional football talent while providing comprehensive support for their professional and personal development. We are committed to ethical practices, transparent dealings, and putting our players' best interests first. Through excellence in training, strategic partnerships, and unwavering dedication, we empower athletes to reach their full potential on and off the field.
